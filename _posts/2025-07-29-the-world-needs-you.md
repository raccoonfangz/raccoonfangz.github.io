> “What we do for ourselves dies with us. What we do for others and the world remains and is immortal.” - <b>Albert Pine</b>

___

<div style="text-align: center; white-space: pre-wrap; font-family: sans-serif;">
<b>World needs you.</b>
It needs your kindness when others are jumping down each other's throats.

<b>Why?</b>

<b>Someone feels less alone because of your kindness.</b>
Maybe they were silently hurting. Your kindness showed them that not everyone is cruel.

**Someone remembers your words in their hardest days.**
Your words were like a warm hug, to which they keep coming back to.

**Someone is trying again because you reminded them they could.**
When doubt took over, your encouragement was the push they needed to keep moving forward.

**Someone carries your advice like a compass in their heart.**
Maybe for you it was obvious, for them it was wisdom they needed.

**Someone feels understood because of something you said.**
When no one else listened or cared, your words made them feel seen and heard.

**Someone learns forgiveness because you showed them grace.**
When anger weighed them down, your example helped them let go.

**Someone keeps going because your faith in them never wavered.**
When they wanted to give up, your belief gave them strength to continue.

<b>You may not always see it, but your presence matters.</b>
More than you will ever know.
</div>
