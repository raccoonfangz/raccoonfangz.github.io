<blockquote style="text-align: center;">
[transl.] The times change, and we change with them
</blockquote>

<hr>

<p align="center">
You are not lost. <br>
You are not broken beyond repair.  <br>
You are still here,   <br>
and that means you still have a choice. <br>
Not to give up, but to fight.   <br>
Fight for what's still here.  <br>
Because while the times change, <br>
we change with them. <br>
And we should not fear time, <br>
instead, we should appreciate it. <br>
Time can fix what doing can’t. <br>
We cannot control everything. <br>
Sometimes being is enough. <br>
Sometimes we just need to wait. <br>
Sometimes waiting is progress. <br>
</p>
