Kindness is the only currency that **never devalues.** In a world where everything is measured by ROI[^1], whether you invest your time, resources or money, **kindness is priceless and its benefits are immeasurable.** While it costs nothing, you become the richest by being kind. \
I know it sounds cliché, but kindness really is contagious. **A simple act of kindness goes a long way.** Your small action can foster the idea in someone to do the same for others! 

**Picture this situation:** Somebody pays you a compliment about your stylish badger socks (yes, it happened to me). 

What’s your first thought? 

**To say thank you and maybe return a compliment back!**

**But what if** someone says something insulting for example: “god, what a fag” -  directed at you while walking down the street? (yes, also speaking from experience)

What’s your first thought there? 

**Probably to insult them back.** Well i didn’t. Not because i didn’t feel like so, but because I did NOT want to be beaten over some stupid thing like that. However I most certainly said some things in my mind.

Kindness is neither purely innate nor purely learned. **However, hate is undeniably learned.[^2]** So instead of hate, spread compassion, understanding and love because kindness may eventually **ripple back to you**, possibly by the very person that received yours. 

[^1]: [abbr.] Return on investment
