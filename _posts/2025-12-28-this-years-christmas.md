Christmas this year for me was genuinely enjoyable. I spent time with my family, played board games, and took part in family gatherings, which made the holidays feel warm and meaningful. Even though I was sick and couldn’t go out with my friends, I still enjoyed staying at home.

I’m still not fully recovered, but one thing I’m proud of is that I studied consistently, except on Christmas Eve. Some people say Christmas should be a time only for rest, but I can rest while still studying. When I have motivation, it makes no sense to waste it.
