> “Sometimes doing nothing is doing 
> something.” \
> **- Wendy Mass**

I’m doing my best. Or am I?

Am I lazy, or just letting myself rest?

Am I finding workouts tough because the weights I used to warm up with now feel heavy, or because I’ve lost the passion I once had for training?

Are my grades better because I genuinely improved, or is it that the subjects got easier?

I could always be more, do more.

So what’s worse?

**Failing without trying**  

or 

**Trying and learning it still wasn’t enough?**
